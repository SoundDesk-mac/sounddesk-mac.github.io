Up to 99 x 2 inputs, 102 x 2 outputs, 16 x 2 auxiliary sends, and 32 I/O busses per desk. Configure channels as mono, stereo, auxiliary receivers, or MIDI instruments—switch seamlessly for any workflow.

Powerful Plugin Hosting

Precise MIDI Control

How It Works

Step 1: Set Up Your Desk

Launch SoundDesk and configure inputs, outputs, and channels. Assign mono, stereo, aux, or MIDI setups to match your sources.

Step 2: Route and Mix

Route any input to outputs, busses, or aux sends. Adjust levels, pans, and effects with pre/post-fader precision.

Step 3: Add Plugins and MIDI

Load Audio Units into slots, connect MIDI controllers, and enable pass-through for virtual instruments.

Step 4: Go Live

Consolidate devices, stream from apps, and monitor in real-time. Export or broadcast your mix effortlessly.
